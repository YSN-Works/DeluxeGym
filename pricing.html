FAQ

Common Questions

Is there any registration or admission fee?

No. We do not charge any registration or admission fee. You only pay for the membership plan you select.

What are the gym timings?

We are open daily from early morning until 12:30 AM, so you can train whenever it suits your schedule.

Can I get a free trial or demo session?

Yes! We offer a free demo session for new visitors. Just message us on WhatsApp and we will schedule it for you.

Do you offer separate timings for ladies?

Yes, we have dedicated ladies timings with female trainers available. Contact us on WhatsApp for the current schedule.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfer, JazzCash and EasyPaisa. A payment receipt is issued for every transaction.

Not Sure Which Plan?

Message us on WhatsApp and our team will recommend the perfect plan for your goals and budget.
